Open source live wallpaper with Malayalam letters streaming down the screen like you have seen in the popular movie Matrix.

Make it yours
* Use your own photo as the background. People and objects in it stay in front of the rain.
* Take the rain color from your photo, or pick any color
* See every change live before setting the wallpaper
* Adjust the text size, tail length, rain density, speed and frame rate
* Depth effect, with some letters falling further back

Having option of choosing characters
* Malayalam Numbers
* Malayalam Binary
* Malayalam Letters
