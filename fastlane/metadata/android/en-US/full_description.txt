Open source live wallpaper with Malayalam letters streaming down the screen like you have seen in the popular movie Matrix.

Having option of choosing characters
* Malayalam Numbers
* Malayalam Binary
* Malayalam Letters

You can also select from 16 Malayalam fonts.
- Manjari
- Gayathri
- Malini
- Nupuram
- Uroob
- Nupuram Calligraphy
- Keraleeyam
- Anjali Old Lipi
- Chilanka
- Dyuthi
- Karumbi
- Meera
- Rachana
- Raghu Malayalam
- Suruma
- Ishtika

Source Code: https://github.com/jishnu7/Akshara-Mazha
Fonts: https://smc.org.in/fonts/
